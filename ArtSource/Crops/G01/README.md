# G01: Blueberry, Raspberry and Blackberry

Current revision: **R05 reference-shaped Rubus blades and restored Blueberry surface.** The supplied Blackberry and Raspberry sprout leaf images are now local under `References`. Their measured asymmetric blade envelopes, base-to-tip axes and width/length ratios are recorded in `Review/Leaf_R05/ReferenceContours.json`. Raw sampled edges are retained alongside fitted small directed teeth, so image raster stair steps are not mistaken for large botanical notches. The resulting mesh has distinct measured species profiles rather than one generic oval.

All 432 planted Rubus blades are closed curved volumes. Their tooth and tip vertices are preserved exactly in the near native meshes; only other surfaces are reduced as necessary to keep the established 26,000-triangle near budget. Distance meshes remain within 2,800 triangles. Source branches, flowers, fruit, seed/harvest geometry and the full Blueberry geometry are retained. Blueberry's detailed pre-R04 color and normal tile is restored pixel-exactly. All 154 atlas allocations and the other 153 surface regions remain unchanged; there is still one runtime plant material.
