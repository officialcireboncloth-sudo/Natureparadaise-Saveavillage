# Field ground transitions and harvestable grass

FieldArea uses terrain-conforming ground surfaces at runtime. Four ground appearances are distinct: terrain path dirt, empty farmable field, dry hoed tiles, and watered hoed tiles. Field Soil Visuals.asset assigns separate pack textures, tint, texture size and smoothness for the three farming states; path dirt belongs to the terrain layers. Its outer boundary has a rounded, irregular feather that reveals the terrain underneath. Hoed cells use smaller rounded stamps with subtle furrows. Wetness is signalled by waterSourcesToday (watering can, rain or sprinkler), with moisture controlling wet intensity. High initial moisture alone does not mark a tile as watered. Fertilizer visuals retain their existing state. Ground surfaces receive shadows but do not cast a rectangular shadow.

Edit Resources/Profiles/Field Soil Visuals.asset to change the farming textures and colors, or assign a custom Soil Visual Profile to an individual field. FieldArea Inspector controls: Blend Ground Surface, Field Edge Feather (default 0.65), Tile Edge Feather (default 0.40), Wild Grass Prefab. The Map field references Resources/World/Wild Grass.prefab, a distinct broad-leaf weed clump from the pack. Harvestable grass retains its stable IDs, sickle action, Grass drops and respawn logic; its collider is separate from the pack visual. Deterministic rotation/scale variation avoids identical clumps.

Hoed tile edges mix dry/wet soil into the actual underlying field texture over 0.40 metres instead of ending at a sharp square. The shader samples the field texture with the same local-metre UV coordinates, tint and texture strength as the base surface. Alpha feather covers only the last 4 cm; the broad transition mixes albedo before lighting, including the furrow detail. Rounded corners and stable world-space noise make the transition slightly irregular. Watered tiles additionally fade wet texture, wet tint and smoothness into dry hoed soil near their edge; the dry soil then fades into field soil. Wet Edge Blend in the soil profile defaults to 0.28 metres. The centre remains clearly wet and tile watering/gameplay data are unchanged. Values are in metres, so the feather does not stretch with field dimensions.

Empty field uses the pack's unlined TFP_Terrain_Dirt_2A_D texture at an 8-metre repeat; Earth textures are reserved for hoed dry/watered cells. Texture Size is measured in field-local metres, so rotation follows the farming grid and changing the number of rows/columns does not stretch the texture. Cell-centre offsets maintain continuous UV coordinates across adjacent stamps, including pooled views. Watered Texture Quarter Turns compensates for different source texture orientations (Earth_1B uses one turn to match Earth_1A); watering must not rotate the soil grooves. Field Texture Strength controls the contrast of the empty-ground pattern without changing the terrain path material.

This is visual mesh/terrain blending, not destructive terrain painting. Runtime meshes conform to terrain heights and transparent textured edges reveal the real ground underneath. Tile data, grid bounds and save schema are unchanged. Greenhouses retain planar floor geometry. The base dirt stays visible at a distance; tile/crop visuals continue using the existing FieldArea distance culling. Existing terrain grass uses its own terrain-detail culling.

Generated meshes and per-field shared materials are released on destruction. Reused tile views regenerate their height sampling when moved. Pack meshes, textures and materials are referenced rather than recolored or overwritten.
