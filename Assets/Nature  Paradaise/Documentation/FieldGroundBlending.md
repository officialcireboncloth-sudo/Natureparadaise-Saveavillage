# Field ground transitions and harvestable grass

FieldArea uses terrain-conforming ground surfaces at runtime. The farming palette retains the original textured brown pack soil with a modest brightness lift, rather than the flat golden/sandy palette. Field Soil Visuals.asset disables the controlled cozy palette so the original RGB texture variation remains visible. Unhoed ground uses tint (1.40, 1.18, 0.91), dry hoed cells use (1.65, 1.42, 1.15), and wet cells remain distinctly darker. Furrow strength returns to 0.09. Terrain path dirt belongs to the terrain layers. The field boundary has a rounded irregular feather that reveals the terrain underneath; hoed cells use smaller rounded stamps with subtle furrows. Surface wetness controls the darkening independently of the daily crop-care flags and survives save/load. Fertilizer visuals retain their existing state. Ground surfaces receive shadows but do not cast a rectangular shadow.

Edit Resources/Profiles/Field Soil Visuals.asset to change the farming palette, or assign a custom Soil Visual Profile to an individual field. FieldArea Inspector controls include Blend Ground Surface, Field Edge Feather, Tile Edge Feather and Wild Grass Prefab. The shared profile supplies the production edge settings. The Map field references Resources/World/Wild Grass.prefab, a distinct broad-leaf weed clump from the pack. Harvestable grass retains its stable IDs, sickle action, Grass drops and respawn logic; its collider is separate from the pack visual. Deterministic rotation/scale variation avoids identical clumps.

Hoed tile edges mix soil into the actual underlying field texture. The shader samples it with the same local-metre UV coordinates, tint and texture strength as the base surface. Alpha feather covers only the last centimetres; the transition mixes albedo before lighting, including the furrow detail. Rounded corners and stable world-space noise make the transition slightly irregular. Watered tiles fade wet tint and smoothness into dry hoed soil over the existing 0.05m wet edge, then into the field soil. The wet multiplier is (0.63, 0.58, 0.53), retaining dark wet soil without crushing the restored texture, with restrained wet smoothness of 0.14. Tile watering/gameplay and drying rules are unchanged. Fresh hoed cells start visually dry in sunny weather, independently of their initial root moisture. Outdoor cells immediately receive rain watering when hoed during drizzle or other rain; this correctly gives them the wet appearance even before using a watering can. Test dry versus watered colours in sunny weather or a weather-protected field. Previously wet saved cells remain wet until normal drying.

Empty field retains its unlined pack dirt texture at the existing 2m repeat. Dry and watered cells now use the same hoed texture at the same 0.8m repeat, with zero wet-texture rotation. Watering changes colour and subtle surface sheen while preserving texture marks, furrow orientation, mesh, rounded outline and blending. Texture Size is measured in field-local metres, so rotation follows the farming grid and changing rows/columns does not stretch it. Cell-centre offsets maintain continuous UV coordinates across adjacent stamps, including pooled views. Soil colour/wetness property blocks are applied only to the hoed ground renderer; plant materials and colours are untouched.

This is visual mesh/terrain blending, not destructive terrain painting. Runtime meshes conform to terrain heights and transparent textured edges reveal the real ground underneath. Tile data, grid bounds and save schema are unchanged. Greenhouses retain planar floor geometry. The base dirt stays visible at a distance; tile/crop visuals continue using the existing FieldArea distance culling. Existing terrain grass uses its own terrain-detail culling.

Generated meshes and per-field shared materials are released on destruction. Reused tile views regenerate their height sampling when moved. Pack meshes, textures and materials are referenced rather than recolored or overwritten.

Hoed stamps now use a Tile Inset of 0.085 per side: their visual width is 83% of the cell spacing, leaving a 17% gap between adjacent stamps (0.136 metres for 0.8-metre cells). The exposed field soil separates dry, watered and planted plots, including upgraded hoe patterns and restored saves. Grid spacing, valid farming area, crop centres, colliders and save data are unchanged. Adjust Tile Inset in Field Soil Visuals.asset to tune this gap without adding geometry or textures.

The field backdrop also extends 0.3 metres beyond each side of the farming grid (Field Border Padding in the soil visual profile). This keeps a continuous unhoed-soil perimeter between the outer grass and dark hoed cells, including corner cells. With 0.8-metre cells and the current inset, the outermost stamp has 0.368 metres of clearance to the backdrop edge before feathering. Field Feather remains 0.12 metres, tile feather remains 0.065 of the cell size, and wet-to-dry blending remains active. Only the existing backdrop mesh dimensions change; the farming collider, grid coordinates, crop placement, UV texture scale and save schema retain their existing values. No additional texture or render pass is introduced.
