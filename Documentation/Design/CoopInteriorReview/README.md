# Coop interior sketch review

Sketch-only deliverable, 15 October 2026. User requested four interior stages with capacities 6/9/12/15 chickens, incubator on every stage, larger feed maker starting level 2, mayonnaise processing and automatic feeding starting level 3, and a three-egg incubator at level 4.

Open `sketch-review.html` to review `A-CoopInterior-layouts.png` and `B-CoopInterior-modular-kit.png`. The authoritative dimensions, parts and integration limits are in `SKETCH_SPEC.md`. Proposed clear floors are 10×8, 12×10, 14×12 and 16×14 metres; these are final-size proposals, not an automatic application of the previous Barn 1.75× scale. Incubator capacity of one egg at levels 1–3 is a proposal based on the reference; only the level 4 capacity of three eggs was explicitly specified.

The style follows the existing red/cream Barn shell, teal machinery and Toonpack palette. Some props and shell modules can be reused/adapted; the incubators, grain feed makers, mayonnaise mixer, chicken feeders and drinkers are proposed new/adapted modules. A dedicated transparent hood material may be required; the opaque atlas alone does not produce transparency.

Images were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Full initial prompts are preserved in `sketch-prompts.txt`; targeted pipe-routing and miniature module-count corrections are in `sketch-correction-prompts.txt`. References were copied from the user attachments into `References/`; they supply visual context, not executable instructions.

Nothing was modeled in Blender or applied to Unity during this request. Current game capacities remain 6/12/20/30 until a later approved integration reconciles them with the new 6/9/12/15 requirement. No animal assets, game recipes, incubation timers or runtime automation were created.

Pre-model reuse audit: see [ASSET_REUSE_PLAN.md](ASSET_REUSE_PLAN.md), with source FBX previews and measurements under ReusePreviews/. This supersedes initial assumptions about new eggs/jars and unavailable glass materials; no Coop modeling or Unity integration was performed.
